# GREENFIELD SCHOOL - Performance Dashboard | Excel

## 📘 Project Overview
Greenfield School has 240 students across 6 classes (JSS1A, JSS1B, JSS2A, JSS2B, JSS3A, JSS3B) operating across three terms (First, Second, Third Term). The school tracks 6 subjects per student. The objective is to analyze academic performance from First Term to Third Term and create a Principal Summary Dashboard to identify struggling classes, hardest subjects, and students who need support.

## ❓ Business Problems Solved
1.  What is the total number of students and the Third Term pass rate?
2.  Which subject is the hardest / lowest-scoring subject overall?
3.  Which class is performing best in Third Term?
4.  How many students scored below 50 and need urgent support?
5.  How has class average trended across the three terms?

## 📊 Key KPIs Delivered
1.  **Total Students:** 240
2.  **Third Term Pass Rate:** 85% (203 out of 240 students passed)
3.  **Hardest Subject Overall:** Mathematics - lowest average score
4.  **Best Class in Third Term:** JSS3B - Highest average
5.  **Students Below 50 (Need Support):** 37 students in Third Term

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Functions: UNIQUE, COUNTA, COUNTIFS, COUNTIF, AVERAGE, IF
- PivotTable & PivotChart
- Data Cleaning & Standardization

## 🖥️ Dashboard Preview
The Principal Summary Dashboard contains:

1.  **KEY NUMBERS Section**
    - Total Students, Pass Rate, Hardest Subject, Best Class, Below 50

2.  **THREE CHARTS**
    - Chart 1: Class Average Trend by Term (Clustered Column) - Shows improvement from First to Third Term
    - Chart 2: Average Score per Subject – Bar Chart (Math’s lowest)
    - Chart 3: Third Term Pass Rate by Class – Clustered Column
   -Top 5 most Improved- Clustered Column 
- Average Subject with Slicers for Term and Class 

3.  **MESSAGE TO PRINCIPAL**
 Based on the analysis of 240 students, Third Term pass rate is 85% (203 students passed). 37 students scored below 50 and need urgent academic support. 
Mathematics is the lowest-scoring subject overall and needs focus. JSS3B is the best performing class in Third Term by average. 
I recommend extra Math’s lessons and to review attendance for the 37 students before final decisions.


## 💡 Key Insights
1.  **37 students scored below 50 in Third Term** - They need immediate academic support and attendance check.
2.  **Mathematics is the hardest subject** - It has the lowest average across all terms and all classes.
3.  **JSS3B is the best performing class** - Consistently highest average in Third Term.
4.  **Overall performance improved** - Average improved from 58 in First Term to 60 in Second Term to 62 in Third Term (Grand Total 60)
5.  **Pass rate is strong but with gaps** - 85% pass rate means most students are doing well, but intervention is needed for the bottom 15%.

## ✅ Conclusion
Based on the data, JSS3B should be used as a model for other classes. The school should focus extra lessons on Mathematics and provide intervention for the 37 students below 50. Before making a big decision, I recommend checking attendance and behavior records for struggling students. The dashboard provides a clear, data-driven view for the Principal to take action.
